Services

With over 30 years of experience, Pallotta Pavers & Concrete stands out for its technical depth of knowledge and unwavering commitment to quality, providing a one-stop solution for a wide array of concrete and hardscape projects.

Every project starts with a free on-site consultation. We look at your grade, your drainage and your soil before quoting, so the number you get is the number you pay.

Pavers

Interlocking pavers, natural stone and travertine, installed on a properly compacted and drained base. A modular surface moves with the ground rather than fighting it, which is why it outlasts poured concrete in Florida soil.

Patios

An interlocking paver patio gives you an outdoor room that holds its shape through Florida heat and heavy summer rain. Individual pavers flex with the ground instead of cracking, and a damaged unit can be lifted and replaced without touching the rest of the surface.

Driveways

Paver driveways carry vehicle loads on a compacted base built to spec, with a contrasting border course that frames the entry. The result is curb appeal that reads as finished work from the street and a surface that will not spider-crack the way a poured slab can.

Hardscapes

Full hardscape design ties patio, walkway, walls and plantings into one plan rather than a set of disconnected projects. We handle grading, drainage and material transitions so the finished space works as a whole.

Steps

Paver and natural stone steps are built to consistent riser heights on a compacted, drained base. Correct geometry is what makes steps feel safe underfoot years after installation.

Retaining Walls

Engineered retaining walls hold grade, create usable level ground and manage water movement across a sloped lot. Every wall is built with proper base preparation, backfill and drainage behind the face.

Pool Decks

Pool decks need a surface that stays cooler underfoot, drains quickly and resists chlorine. Pavers and travertine do all three, and the modular surface can be lifted for pool or plumbing access without demolition.

Fire Pits

A built-in fire pit turns a patio into a space you use after dark and through the cooler months. We build them in matching or contrasting block so the feature reads as part of the original design.

Also offered

Commercial Pavers

Entries, courtyards and drive aprons built to carry continuous traffic. Modular paving lets a section be lifted and repaired without closing the whole area.

Concrete

Forming, pouring and finishing, from a garage floor to a commercial parking area. We use above-industry-standard mixes and place control joints deliberately, so the slab behaves the way it was designed to.

RV Pads

RV and boat pads are poured at the thickness and reinforcement your load actually requires, on a base compacted to prevent settling. Correct slope keeps water off the pad and away from the structure.

Driveways

Poured concrete driveways with a compacted base, correct thickness for vehicle loads and saw-cut joints on a deliberate grid. Finish options run from broom to decorative.

Sidewalks

Walkways, sidewalks and entry landings poured to consistent width and slope, with the cross-fall needed to shed water rather than pond it against the house.

Seamless Texture Concrete

Seamless texture and stamped finishes give a poured slab the look of stone or tile with no joint lines to trap dirt. Applied while the concrete is still workable, then sealed.

Also offered

Garage Floors

Garage floors placed and finished flat, with control joints laid out to direct any future shrinkage cracking to planned lines rather than across the middle of the floor.

Pole Barn / Metal Building Slabs

Slabs for pole barns and metal buildings, formed to the building footprint with anchor placement, thickened edges and reinforcement coordinated to the structure going on top.

Parking Lots

Commercial parking areas poured for sustained vehicle loading, with jointing, drainage fall and reinforcement planned before the first truck arrives.
